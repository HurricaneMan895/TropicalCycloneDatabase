Data across the TROPICAL CYCLONE DATABASE is powered by the Atlantic Hurricane Database (HURDAT2, 1851–2025) for all landfall positions, wind, and pressure. The database was also utilized to recover landfalls HURDAT2 didn't flag. A landfall counts if a storm’s center crosses a coastline at tropical storm strength or above. This website is built as a reference and educational tool, so as always, confirm figures/decisions against the National Hurricane Center’s official reports.
